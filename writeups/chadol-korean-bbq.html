Chadol Korean BBQ

4.0 Korean BBQ · Premium Cuts

The original Chadol location, and still the standard-bearer. Every table comes with a built-in grill, and the cuts here start a notch above what you'll find elsewhere — Certified Angus beef and top-2%-grade Duroc pork, cooked tableside exactly how you like it.

What to Order

The brisket and the galbi are the two orders regulars won't let you skip. Pair them with the usual spread of banchan, and don't rush the grill — letting the fat render slowly is where the flavor actually comes from.

The Verdict

4 out of 5 gems. A reliable, high-quality Korean BBQ spot that earns its reputation as the original — premium cuts, done right, without needing to dress it up.
